How to play

1. Turn the mirrors. Tap a round mirror to flip it. Sound bounces off them.

2. Release the sound. It travels in straight lines. Walls stop it.

3. Wake the star target (gold ring) but not the sleepers. Wake a sleeper and you try again.

X splitter sends sound both ways. Range means the sound fades after that many tiles.

Deep sleepers (blue cap, badge like ≤4) only wake if the sound reaches them within that many tiles. Fainter sound slips right past them.

Stars: solve with the fewest taps (par) for three. Hints are free but limited. Stars unlock new wave colors.

Privacy

Noise Smuggler by Adeba Studio does not collect, send or sell any personal data.

Your progress (stars, records, settings) is saved only in your own browser using local storage. Clearing your browser data, or using Erase my progress in Settings, removes it. You can export a backup file in Settings and import it on any device.

The game makes no network requests while you play, uses no analytics, no cookies and no accounts, and never asks for the microphone. All sounds are generated on your device. The only link that leaves the game is the optional Support link, which opens only if you tap it.

If advertising or purchases are added in a future platform release, this page will be updated before that release.
